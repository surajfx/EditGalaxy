Post #9 / Still Among Motion ✨

CinematicEasyChatGPT

This prompt works great with ChatGPT image generation. Copy & paste and create your image!

What this prompt is useful for

This prompt is built for ai image generation and creative edits, and works best with ChatGPT. Final images typically suit Instagram / TikTok / YouTube in 4:5 / 9:16 / 1:1 format.

How to use this prompt

  1. Copy the prompt text below (choose the girl or boy version, whichever fits).
  2. Open your AI image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Leonardo AI, or similar).
  3. Paste the prompt, and upload a clear reference photo if the prompt calls for one.
  4. Generate the image, then try a second generation if the first result needs adjusting.
  5. Download and share your result.

👩 Girl Version

A high fashion magazine pictorial of an Alpine-looking woman in her early 20s who stands still, looking at the camera, turning her head in the center, surrounded by a blurry silhouette of people passing by. She has a calm, stoic look on her face and is wearing modern, elegant attire. Bright, abstract, and emphasizes the contrast between motion blur and static appearance. EDITING STYLE: Pure art photography, high fashion, film composition, soft magazine texture, high contrast, high resolution. FOCUS: motion blur around the subject sharp, clear focus on the woman dramatic light & shadow expressive color contrast MOOD: Calm, powerful, timeless, visually striking CATEGORY: GIRLS 4:5 RATIO

Customization tips

  • Swap specific words (colours, outfit, setting) in the prompt to match your own idea.
  • If the result doesn't look right, try regenerating — AI image tools can vary between runs.
  • Upload a clear, well-lit reference photo if the prompt asks you to use one.

Important image-generation tips

  • Use a high-resolution reference photo for the most accurate result.
  • Different AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Leonardo AI) can render the same prompt differently — try more than one if you're not happy with the first result.
  • Some tools may need the prompt split into two messages if it's long; paste it in full first and only split if it's rejected.
