What Are CapCut Templates?

CapCut templates are ready-made video editing designs that help you create videos without editing everything manually.

They come with pre-designed transitions, effects, music timing, animations, and video arrangements. Instead of starting from a blank editing screen, you can simply select a template and replace its sample media with your own photos or video clips.

CapCut templates are especially useful for beginners and content creators who want to make attractive videos quickly and easily.

How to Find Your Favorite Template

Finding a template on EditGalaxy is very easy.

Step 1: Open All Templates

Tap the All Templates option to explore the complete collection of available CapCut templates.

Step 2: Browse the Collection

Scroll through the template cards and check the preview images to find an editing style you like.

Step 3: Use the Search Box

Looking for a particular template? Enter its template number or a related keyword in the search box.

Step 4: Choose Your Favorite

Once you find the template you want, open its card and check the available details.

Step 5: Tap Use Template on CapCut

Tap the Use Template on CapCut button to open the selected template in the CapCut app.
