Ready-to-copy AI image prompts

Each prompt on EditGalaxy is written to work with popular AI image tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Leonardo AI. Choose a prompt, copy it, upload your own photo if the prompt asks for one and generate your image.

How to use a prompt

Open a prompt, tap Get Prompt, pick the girl or boy version, copy the text and paste it into your AI tool. If the first result is not perfect, generate again or change a few words such as colours, outfit or background.

Tips for better images

Use a high-resolution reference photo, keep faces clearly visible, and try the same prompt in more than one AI tool because each one renders styles differently.
